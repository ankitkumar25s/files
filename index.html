What we fix

Punctures and tyres

Tube patching, tube replacement and tyre changes for road, hybrid and kids' cycles. Usually done while you wait.

Brakes and gears

Cable adjustment, pad replacement and gear tuning so the cycle stops when you want it to and shifts without crunching.

Full overhaul

We strip it down, clean and re-grease every bearing, true the wheels and put it back together. Takes two days.

About the shop

Gear & Chain has been fixing cycles in Vijay Nagar since 2009. It started as a one-table puncture stall outside a school gate and is now a proper workshop with two mechanics.

Most of our customers are students, daily commuters and people who bought a cycle during lockdown and have only just realised it needs looking after. We explain what is wrong before we touch anything, and we tell you if a repair isn't worth the money.
